Founder · CEO · Chief Network Security Engineer

I founded VALO Technologies LLC after years of working inside high-stakes environments — Department of Defense networks at BAMC, statewide infrastructure at Texas HHSC, banking, and Dallas County government. The pattern was always the same: smart organizations running misconfigured firewalls, sitting on bloated rulebases, and one bad change away from an outage or a breach.

The shift to a Palo Alto Networks-centered practice was deliberate. PA-Series firewalls and Panorama are the platform serious organizations actually trust — and the gap between deploying them and deploying them correctly is enormous. That's the gap we close.
