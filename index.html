How a change is engineered

The cutover should be the uneventful part.

The work that protects a change window happens before it: knowing what the current firewall actually does, staging the replacement, and agreeing how to back out.

  1. 01ScopeInventory current policy, NAT and dependencies. Agree the change window and rollback before work starts.
  2. 02StageTranslate and stage the new configuration with the rollback plan ready.
  3. 03Cut overShift approved traffic inside the agreed window.
  4. 04Validate & hand overTest what should pass and what should not, then hand over the documentation.
