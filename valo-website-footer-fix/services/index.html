Services

Security and compliance leadership, security engineering, and offensive security testing. Four service lines designed to be combined or run independently.

VALO Technologies is a security-first consultancy. We don't sell managed IT, hardware, or telecom. We sell security and compliance leadership, security engineering, and offensive security testing. That focus is intentional.

Most of our clients already have IT support. What they need is the layer above and around IT: someone accountable for the security program, someone who speaks the language of HIPAA or GLBA or the IRS, someone who can stand in front of an auditor or a board and explain what controls exist and why.

Our four service lines are designed to be combined or run independently, depending on what you need.

Virtual CISO (vCISO)
Strategic security leadership on retainer. We own your risk register, develop and maintain your policies, prepare you for audits, and report to your executive team and board. The CISO function without the full-time hire.
Learn more
Compliance & Risk
HIPAA, SOC 2, and CMMC readiness, gap assessments, and audit preparation. We translate regulatory requirements into operational controls your team can actually implement.
Learn more
Network Security Engineering
Senior engineering depth across Palo Alto Networks (NGFW and Prisma Cloud), Microsoft Defender and Sentinel, and Cisco Umbrella. Architecture, design, and deployment work most consultancies recommend but don't actually do — across on-premises network security and multi-cloud workload protection.
Learn more
Penetration Testing & Vulnerability Assessment
External, internal, web application, and social engineering testing aligned to PTES and NIST SP 800-115. Findings you can act on, not a scan report dressed up as a pen test.
Learn more
How engagements work

How engagements typically combine

Most clients start with a risk assessment to understand where they actually stand. From there, the path forks. Organizations that need ongoing security leadership move into a vCISO retainer. Organizations facing a specific regulatory requirement engage us on a focused compliance project. Organizations with technical security gaps engage our network security engineering or penetration testing services. The four service lines are the building blocks; the engagement is shaped to your situation.
