About the founder

Loseini "Valo" Kamara is a cybersecurity professional with 9+ years of experience in network security and infrastructure engineering across healthcare, financial services, and government.

He holds a Master of Science in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance and an MBA in Information Technology Management.

He founded VALO Technologies to provide specialized cybersecurity engineering and consulting to managed service providers (MSPs), small and midsize businesses (SMBs), and state, local, and education (SLED) organizations.
