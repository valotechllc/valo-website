Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

VALO Technologies LLC ("VALO," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website at valotechnologiellc.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when you use our Site.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

When you submit a contact form on our Site, we collect the information you enter, which may include your name, organization name, email address, phone number, industry, a description of what prompted your inquiry, and how you heard about us. This information is transmitted to us via Formspree (our form processing service) and delivered to our email.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our Site, standard web server logs and analytics tools may collect information such as your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and time and date of your visit. This Site does not currently deploy third-party analytics scripts (e.g., Google Analytics). If that changes, this policy will be updated before deployment.

Cookies

This Site does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies. The Formspree service may set cookies as part of form processing. Please review Formspree's privacy policy for their data practices.

How We Use Your Information

We use information you submit through our contact form to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Schedule and conduct a consultation call
  • Assess whether VALO Technologies is an appropriate fit for your needs
  • Send follow-up communications related to your inquiry

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not use your contact information for unsolicited marketing to unrelated organizations.

How We Store and Protect Your Information

Information submitted through our contact form is transmitted through Formspree's infrastructure and delivered to our business email. We treat all information you provide as confidential. Employees and contractors who access this information do so only as necessary to respond to your inquiry.

No transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted through our Site.

Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following third-party services in connection with this Site:

  • Formspree — form submission processing. Formspree receives the data you enter in our contact form and transmits it to us. Review Formspree's privacy policy at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Google Fonts — web font delivery. Google may log requests for font files. Review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting. GitHub may log access requests. Review GitHub's privacy policy at docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and related correspondence for as long as necessary to manage our business relationships and comply with applicable legal obligations. Inquiries that do not result in an engagement are generally retained for up to two years.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have submitted to us by contacting us at info@valotechnologiellc.com. We will respond to reasonable requests within 30 days.

If you are a resident of California or another jurisdiction with specific consumer privacy rights, additional rights may apply. Please contact us for more information.

Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have received information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

Links to Other Sites

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
