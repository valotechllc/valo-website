Privacy Policy

How VALO Technologies handles information submitted through this website.

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Information we collect

VALO Technologies LLC may collect information you choose to provide through website forms or direct communications, including your name, organization, email address, phone number, industry, service interests, referral source, newsletter subscription information, and the content of your message.

The website may also collect limited technical and usage information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, approximate location derived from network information, and referral information through analytics technologies.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, evaluate whether VALO is a fit for the requested work, prepare or discuss potential engagements, deliver the VALO Security Brief to people who subscribe, maintain business records, improve the website, and protect the security and integrity of our systems and services.

We do not sell personal information obtained through this website.

Website service providers

Website inquiry forms are processed using Formspree. Website traffic may be measured using Google Analytics. These providers may process technical or submitted information according to their own terms and privacy practices. VALO may also use ordinary business technology providers for email, hosting, security, and communications.

Cookies and analytics

The site may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics, security, and basic website functionality. Browser settings can be used to limit or remove cookies, although doing so may affect some website features.

Information sharing

We may share information with service providers that support website hosting, form processing, analytics, email, security, or other business operations. We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, systems, users, or the public.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate business needs, or to meet legal and contractual requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-party links

This website may link to third-party websites or services. VALO is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties.

Your choices

You may contact us to request correction or deletion of personal information you previously submitted through the website, subject to applicable legal, contractual, security, and record-retention requirements. Newsletter subscribers may unsubscribe at any time by replying to a newsletter or contacting info@valotechnologiellc.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website, services, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to info@valotechnologiellc.com.
