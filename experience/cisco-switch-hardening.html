Enterprise Cisco Switch Security Hardening

Management-plane security improvements driven by Rapid7 vulnerability findings and confirmed through follow-up scanning.

Professional experience disclosure

This project was completed by VALO Technologies' founder during previous employment. It is presented as individual professional experience, not as a client engagement or contract performed by VALO Technologies LLC.

Cisco Catalyst & IOS Rapid7 SNMPv3 Advanced ACLs

The challenge

A Rapid7 vulnerability assessment identified weaknesses affecting enterprise Cisco switch management configurations, including enabled HTTP/HTTPS web-management services, legacy SNMPv2 settings, and other management-plane security exposures.

Project ownership

As a Senior Network Security Engineer during previous employment, VALO's founder owned the switch hardening initiative, reviewing the identified findings and implementing corrective configuration changes across affected devices.

Remediation approach

  1. Remove unneeded web-management services. Disable HTTP and HTTPS management services on the affected switches to reduce exposed management functionality.
  2. Modernize the monitoring protocol. Remove legacy SNMPv2 configurations and deploy SNMPv3 for network monitoring and management.
  3. Restrict management-plane access. Apply advanced access control lists (ACLs) to switch management interfaces to reduce unauthorized access to management services.
  4. Validate remediation. Perform follow-up Rapid7 vulnerability scanning to confirm that the affected findings were remediated.

Outcome

Implemented the required management-plane hardening measures and confirmed remediation of the affected vulnerabilities through follow-up Rapid7 scans. The work demonstrates network device security hardening, direct configuration ownership, and validation against identified findings.

Relevant capabilities

Cisco Catalyst switching, Cisco IOS, switch management-plane protection, advanced ACLs, HTTP/HTTPS service hardening, SNMPv3 migration, Rapid7 vulnerability assessment, and remediation verification.
