How engagements work

How engagements typically combine

MSPs may engage VALO for one firewall migration, policy cleanup, or troubleshooting project. SMB and SLED clients may begin with a network security or cybersecurity risk assessment, then decide whether to proceed with remediation. Other advisory services remain available when needed.

Every project has a defined scope, deliverables, and handover. We do not require ongoing managed-service commitments.
