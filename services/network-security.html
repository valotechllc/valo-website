Network Infrastructure & Security Engineering

The network your practice runs on, built to satisfy the auditor from day one.

Most firms make you choose

A network engineer who builds fast but leaves you to worry about compliance later. Or a security consultant who audits the network but can't touch the hardware. VALO does both in one engagement — we design, migrate, and troubleshoot the infrastructure your day runs on, and we build it so uptime, security, and HIPAA or PCI requirements are handled together, not bolted on after.

That combination is the point. A pure network engineer can't tell you whether your segmentation satisfies the HIPAA Security Rule. A pure compliance consultant can't reconfigure the switch. We do.

What we do

Design & Migration
Switch refreshes & cutovers

Cisco and Arista switch configuration, refreshes, and migrations — scoped and priced so you know the cost before we start, with tested cutovers that minimize downtime.

Segmentation
Access control mapped to compliance

VLAN design and network segmentation that isolates the systems handling patient or cardholder data — mapped directly to HIPAA and PCI-DSS requirements, and documented for audit evidence.

Troubleshooting
Performance & reliability

Diagnosis and resolution for networks that can't afford downtime — intermittent drops, slow performance, failing hardware, and the problems your current support hasn't been able to pin down.

Security Posture
Network security review

Firewall and ACL posture, default credentials, wireless security, remote access, and logging — the network-level security layer, delivered alongside the infrastructure work rather than as a separate invoice.

The Network Assessment

The simplest way to start. A fixed-scope, on-site assessment of your network against three lenses — reliability, security, and HIPAA gaps. You walk away with a plain-language report: prioritized findings, the compliance gaps called out specifically, and a remediation roadmap any qualified provider could execute.

No obligation to use us for the remediation. The assessment stands on its own — if you never call us again, you still own a clear picture of your network and what to fix first.

How the assessment works

Five phases, scoped to a single site. Most small-practice assessments are completed within about a week start to finish.

01
Scoping & authorization — We confirm exactly what's in scope and obtain written authorization before any access. Nothing gets touched that you haven't approved.
02
On-site discovery — We inventory your switches, firewalls, and access points, review configuration and topology, and document the physical environment.
03
Analysis — We score the environment for reliability, security, and whether the network design supports your HIPAA obligations.
04
Report & roadmap — Prioritized findings by severity, compliance gaps mapped to the HIPAA Security Rule, and a vendor-neutral remediation roadmap.
05
Readout — A live walkthrough of the findings so you understand what they mean and what to prioritize — not just a PDF in your inbox.
Honest scope

VALO works in Cisco and Arista environments and serves SMB and clinic-scale networks. Large multi-site campus builds and carrier-grade work are outside our current scope. If your network isn't a fit, we'll tell you on the scoping call rather than stretch to take the work.

Pricing for remediation work is scope-specific — a single switch swap and a full segmentation rebuild are very different engagements. The assessment itself is a fixed, flat fee, quoted up front on a short scoping call so the number reflects your site, not a generic rate card.
