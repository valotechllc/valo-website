Background

Security work that actually reduces risk — not just checks boxes.

I started Valo Technologies because I kept seeing the same problem: small organizations — clinics, practices, local businesses — were either being ignored by large security firms or oversold expensive tools they didn't need and couldn't operate.

With over a decade in cybersecurity and network engineering, I've worked across firewall operations, secure network architecture, vulnerability management, and compliance programs. I've held roles that required me to translate complex risk into language executives could act on — and then go execute it.

At Valo, I do the same thing — just directly for the clients who need it most. No layers. No account managers. You work with me, and I'm accountable for every deliverable.

The name Valo means light in Finnish. That's the job: bringing clarity to environments where risk is hidden, compliance is confusing, and the path forward isn't obvious.
