Sample Network & Security Assessment

A proof-of-work example showing how VALO evaluates network reliability, segmentation, firewall controls, and remediation priorities.

Fictional demonstration

This is not a client case study. The organization, systems, findings, metrics, and risk values below are fictional and exist only to demonstrate the structure and level of detail of a VALO deliverable.

Executive context

Example organization: Oak Ridge Family Clinic, a fictional single-site clinic with 55 users, one next-generation firewall, three managed switches, nine wireless access points, two internet circuits, six VLANs, and cloud-hosted business applications.

Objective: assess reliability, segmentation, firewall/access controls, administrative exposure, wireless isolation, core services, and HIPAA-relevant network risks.

Sample findings

IDFindingPriorityImpactRecommended action
N-01Clinical workstations and general business endpoints share an overly broad trusted VLAN.HighCompromise of one endpoint increases lateral-movement exposure.Design role-based segmentation and restrict inter-VLAN flows to documented business requirements.
N-02Firewall rulebase contains stale any/any service objects and rules without current business owners.HighUnnecessary network paths increase attack surface and weaken accountability.Validate rule ownership, remove stale rules, narrow services, and document recurring review.
N-03Network-device administration is reachable from standard user networks.HighUser-endpoint compromise could expose network management interfaces.Create a management segment and restrict administration by source, protocol, and authorized identities.
N-04Guest wireless isolation is incomplete from selected internal RFC1918 ranges.HighGuest devices may reach internal services not intended for public access.Enforce firewall isolation, validate ACLs, and retest from guest clients.
N-05Configuration backups are manual and not verified after network changes.ModerateRecovery from hardware failure or configuration error may be delayed.Automate configuration backup and periodically test restore procedures.

Example architecture priorities

0–30 days

  • Restrict management-plane access.
  • Correct guest-network isolation.
  • Review high-risk firewall rules.
  • Capture current configs and topology.

31–60 days

  • Implement segmentation changes.
  • Standardize switch management controls.
  • Improve configuration backup.
  • Review DNS/DHCP dependencies.

61–90 days

  • Retest segmentation.
  • Validate failover process.
  • Document network standards.
  • Close findings with evidence.

Flat-fee qualification illustrated

This fictional environment fits the published $2,500 package because it is one small site with a limited device inventory and conventional architecture. A multi-site estate, data center, SD-WAN environment, large switch/AP population, complex BGP/OSPF, NAC/ISE deployment, cloud transit architecture, or major redesign would be custom-scoped instead.
