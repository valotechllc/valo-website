Sample HIPAA Security Risk Analysis

A proof-of-work example showing how VALO can document healthcare security risk, safeguard gaps, ownership, and remediation priorities.

Fictional demonstration

This is not a client case study. The organization, systems, findings, metrics, and risk values below are fictional and exist only to demonstrate the structure and level of detail of a VALO deliverable.

Executive context

Example organization: Lakeside Behavioral Health, a fictional 42-employee outpatient behavioral-health practice using a cloud EHR, Microsoft 365, managed endpoints, a small office network, telehealth, and eight business associates.

Objective: identify reasonably anticipated risks to ePHI, document safeguard gaps, assign risk ownership, and produce a practical remediation plan.

11material risks documented
4high-priority risks
8critical vendors reviewed
90 daysinitial remediation horizon

Sample findings

IDFindingRiskSafeguard areaRecommended action
H-01ePHI inventory does not fully document all storage locations, exports, telehealth workflows, and local device handling.HighRisk analysis / asset visibilityComplete an ePHI data-flow and asset inventory; assign ownership and review it annually and after material changes.
H-02MFA is not consistently enforced for all remote and privileged access paths.HighAccess control / authenticationEnforce MFA, remove legacy paths, document emergency access, and validate privileged-account separation.
H-03Backup restoration is assumed but has not been demonstrated through a documented recovery test.HighContingency planningPerform a recovery exercise, capture evidence, define recovery ownership, and track deficiencies.
H-04Business associate oversight is inconsistent and security evidence is not tracked centrally.ModerateThird-party riskMaintain a business-associate inventory, BAA status, risk tier, security evidence, renewal dates, and remediation items.
H-05Known security findings lack target dates and formal risk acceptance.ModerateSecurity management processUse a risk register with owner, due date, treatment, exception, residual risk, and leadership review.

Sample remediation roadmap

0–30 days

  • Complete ePHI inventory and data flows.
  • Enforce MFA for privileged and remote access.
  • Assign owners to all high risks.
  • Document critical business associates and BAA status.

31–60 days

  • Run backup restoration test.
  • Review privileged access and account lifecycle.
  • Formalize risk acceptance and exception process.
  • Update incident contacts and escalation paths.

61–90 days

  • Run a security incident tabletop.
  • Validate remediation evidence.
  • Review vendor security evidence.
  • Present residual risks to leadership.

What the client would receive

Executive risk analysis report, HIPAA-aligned risk register, safeguard-gap summary, third-party observations, prioritized remediation roadmap, evidence requests, and a leadership readout. The engagement does not represent a legal opinion, certification, or regulatory guarantee.
